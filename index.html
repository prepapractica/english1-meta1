Prepare to Read: The Metamorphosis[cite: 1]

Welcome to the PrepaPráctica self-reading check. Franz Kafka was one of the greatest German-speaking writers of the twentieth century, but he received no support from his family[cite: 1]. His mother did not understand his passion for literature, while his father, a man obsessed with materialism, would not accept "author" as a legitimate profession[cite: 1]. Kafka was torn between multiple social worlds; his German identity clashed constantly with his Jewish heritage[cite: 1].
